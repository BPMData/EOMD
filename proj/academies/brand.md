## Pathways (3 per academy)
- **Emerging Technology & Entrepreneurship:** Business & Entrepreneurship · Investment & Finance · Medical Technology & Engineering
- **Scientific Innovation & Medicine:** Biomedical · Sports & Human Performance · Allied Health Services
- **Clean Energy & Green Design:** Clean & Renewable Energy · Green Design & Environmental Stewardship · Sustainable Food & Living Systems
- **Information Technology & Cybersecurity:** Networking · Computer Programming & Gaming · Cybersecurity
- **Professional & Public Service:** Justice & Law · Education & Human Development · Culinary Arts & Hospitality Management
- **Art, Engineering & Design:** Art, Architecture & Civil Engineering · Computer Integrated Manufacturing · Applied Engineering
- **Communication & Design:** Broadcasting & Multimedia · Audio Engineering & Design · Graphic & Digital Design

## Rules
- Pathways are open to all students and assigned by lottery regardless of grades. Students **rank** choices for grades 10–12. Never say "choose" or "pick," and never promise placement.
- All 9th graders start in Exploration Academy.
- Dark text on yellow. Keep all text AA-legible (large bold text only on the orange).
- Don't invent facts, stats, dates, URLs or facilities.
