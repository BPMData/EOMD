# SSR poster: brief and decisions
Smart board, Sustained Silent Reading, 15–20 min, students reading silently, many English learners (Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese).
Source copy: `proj/ssr/poster-source.html` (used word for word).

## Decisions
- **Look.** Kells-leaning living poster: flat color, curling outlined vines, spirals, eye-buds (Bryan loves them), spiral roundels; deep teal ↔ indigo background shifting over 90 s.
  - Rejected: field journal (too literal); Matisse fronds (the scale-in read as phallic); CD-ROM (parked for a separate piece).
- **Copy kept.** All instructional lines and the ES/PT subtitles, plus "Lectura silentium quotidianum · the daily silent reader" (Bryan likes the quotidian joke).
- **Copy dropped** (field-guide chrome): CLASSROOM FIELD GUIDE · DAILY OBSERVATION LOG · NO. 014; the Latin creature names; "Field marks:"; "Observed daily. Logged weekly."; FIELD GUIDE ED. 2026.
- **Music.** Direction A, "TV science segment" (Rhodes/vibes, 66 BPM, −23 LUFS). Direction B was rejected: the flute is too distracting.
- **Behavior.** One text card at a time, cycling in order, ~13 s fully open. Growth must never pile up (occupancy grid).
- **Timer.** Default 20 min; chime + TICKET OUT card at the end; the chime plays even when the music is muted.
