Channel D: two text-mode options

Both run on the same 72×24 character grid at 44 px (the body-text minimum), so everything, including the cards, is real text-mode type. Headlines are double-size (90 px). Each Dud is a live sim: it wanders, gets bonked by words (angry face), startles when it bumps another Dud, cheers at corner hits, and one stops to speak each card. Card timing is shortened here (8 s up, 5 s off). Cards now use the 16-color text-mode palette with chunky pixel headlines. Turn sound on to hear the speaking Dud: Star Fox-style blips locked to a 112 BPM grid and a pentatonic scale, with pitch and wave set by his mutation color, played through the DOS PC-speaker filter.
