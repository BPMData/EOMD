# Concepts: CSE hallway loop

Stage: concepts · made from the approved brief only (no tools opened). Choice panel: `choices-concepts.html`.
On-screen words in the sketches are **DRAFT**.

**The one sentence:** even if you've never coded, in CSE you build real things.
**Clichés off the table:** binary rain, green terminal text, hoodie hackers, glowing circuit boards, light bulbs,
gears, brains, robots with antennas, "the future", "tomorrow's innovators", padlocks.

## A. Snap (recommended)
- **The idea:** colorful code blocks snap together in mid-air, and every stack turns into the thing it runs.
- **What they'd see:** a bright glass lobby. Three blocks click into a stack; the stack folds open into a phone app
  (MIT App Inventor). Blocks snap again; they unroll into a road and a VEXcode robot car drives it. Then the blocks
  slide aside to show the same code as Python text: the course's own path from blocks to text, shown in one move. A
  red Academy passage: blocks light up a micro:bit's grid of lights, then lift a CoDrone EDU into the air. The drone
  drops a new block and the loop begins again, mid-motion.
- **How it feels:** playful, clicky, "I could do that." Steers away from dense code and anything nerdy.
- **Sound:** a bouncy, bright groove where every snap lands on the beat with a soft wooden click.
- **Why I recommend it:** blocks are exactly how a beginner starts in CSE, so the picture itself says "no experience
  needed." Each beat stands alone (one stack, one thing), and it belongs to this course and nothing else.

## B. Ground Floor
- **The idea:** CSE is the lobby of a sleek glass tower, the floor everyone in the Academy starts on, and each floor
  above holds something you'll build.
- **What they'd see:** your Neo-New York glass lobby, for real. The camera rides a glass elevator up past lit floors:
  apps, robot cars, Python, then two red Academy floors (micro:bit, drones). At the top, the Academy's three pathways
  as three glass hexagons. The top floor's reflection becomes the lobby floor, so the ride never ends.
- **How it feels:** sleek, aspirational, calm-confident. Steers away from cartoony.
- **Sound:** a smooth, rising pulse, glassy keys, a little lift at each floor.
- **Trade-off:** most "neocorp lobby", and it shows CSE leads to all three pathways; but a tower is closer to the
  corporate look, so it has to work hard to stay fun.

## C. One Line
- **The idea:** a single bright line runs the whole loop, drawing everything students make as it goes.
- **What they'd see:** on a light screen, a line races in and becomes a road (the robot car follows it), bends into a
  phone outline (an app blinks on), writes a line of Python, traces the micro:bit's lights, then becomes the drone's
  flight path, which loops back to the start.
- **How it feels:** clean, quick, clever. Steers away from clutter.
- **Sound:** a light, plucky melody that follows the line like a pen.
- **Trade-off:** the most elegant, but the most abstract: a passer-by sees a drawing more than "I could make this."
