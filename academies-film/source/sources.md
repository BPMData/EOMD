# Sources

Every fact on screen comes from the project brief supplied by Bryan Murphy (Danbury HS), September 2026. That includes the academy names, pathway names, colors, campuses and addresses, the lottery and ranking process, and the facility nods (the stock ticker for EmTech and the skylight lab for Clean Energy).

Nothing was added from outside sources. There are no statistics, dates, URLs, or deadlines on screen.

Before posting, please re-check the academy and pathway names against the current Program of Studies.
