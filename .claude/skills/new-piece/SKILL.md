---
name: new-piece
description: Start any new EOMD piece (video, promo, poster, player, screensaver, or a cartridge made for an audience) by interviewing the client and getting a brief approved before any design or build. Use whenever someone asks for a new piece, however short the request. Not for changes to an existing piece.
---
Follow `brief/INTERVIEW.md` from the top: interview in rounds (the ★ questions every time, the mode question in round 1), write `proj/<name>/brief.md` from `brief/TEMPLATE.md`, get it approved, then concepts, then design before tools. `brief/example-academies.md` is the level of detail to aim for.
