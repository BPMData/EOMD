EOMD

Efficient Offline Motion Designer: motion graphics and synthesized music in single HTML files. Click one to play it; press Start sound for the music, move the mouse for the controls.

Players

Gen playerGenerative motion cartridges. Press G for the gallery, K for the knobs. JukeboxEvery music cartridge: lounge, film scores, study loops, chip music. CS Lab ScreensaverFive retro computer worlds, chip music, trilingual coding-tip cards. CS Lab Screensaver: AmbientThe same worlds without the tip cards. Living posterVines grow and write words in cursive, with a reading soundtrack. Living poster: CSWith trilingual (EN/ES/PT) computer-science tip cards. Living poster: Exploration SeminarWith trilingual cards for a 9th-grade seminar. The Academies of Danbury High SchoolA 60-second seamless hallway film with its own soundtrack.

Make your own

Clone the repo and point your AI coding assistant at it: github.com/BPMData/EOMD. The README explains how to build players and turn any of them into an MP4.
